Why this is safe

100% Client-Side Processing: This application runs entirely in memory within your web browser. Your pupil report PDFs are never uploaded to our server or any third-party storage. Everything is read and parsed locally on your device.

Strict Multi-Layer Anonymisation: All identifying information is scrubbed before any message is built. Pupil full names, first names, surnames, teacher names, Unique Pupil Numbers (UPN), Unique Learner Numbers (ULN), dates of birth, candidate numbers, school names, emails, phone numbers, and postcodes are systematically extracted into a local private key and replaced with anonymous tokens (such as P01, P02).

Strict Privacy Guard with Smart Name Review: Before any message leaves your device, automated security scans inspect the text. Any actual identifying student or staff details (pupil names from reports, teacher names, UPNs, dates of birth, contact details) are strictly blocked from leaving. If the scan spots a possible name that belongs to question text or curriculum material (such as "Alice has 3 watermelons" or an author name), it highlights the exact sentence and warns you, giving you the choice to review the context and send anyway using your professional judgement.

Pre-Flight Network Interceptor: Directly before any network call can be made, a hard safety assertion runs at the browser's network fetch layer. If an identifying token is ever detected, execution is immediately aborted before any bytes can leave your computer.

Restricted Network Boundaries (CSP): A strict Content Security Policy limits all external connections exclusively to authorized AI endpoints, with strict no-referrer privacy enabled.

Local Key Isolation: The translation key linking pupil codes back to names remains strictly inside your local browser session. When the AI finishes its analysis, your browser locally restores the names on your screen so you can read the report without risk.

If this page breaks later

This website should be self sufficient for a reasonable amount of time. It is meant to keep working on its own, on the teacher’s computer, without anyone having to look after it day to day.

At some point OpenRouter or Pupil Progress will inevitablly change the format of something in a way that breaks this website. That is a normal risk with a tool that takes input from someone else’s PDFs and talks to someone else’s AI for whitch we hav no control over.
